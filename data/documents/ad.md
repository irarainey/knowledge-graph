# Airworthiness Directive — Seat Rail Inspection (Summary)

Reference: AD applicable to Cessna single-engine seat rails and locking mechanisms.

## Applicability
All Cessna 100-series aircraft fitted with pilot and co-pilot seats on roller-type rails.

## Requirement
1. Inspect the seat rails for wear, cracks, and elongation of the locking-pin holes.
2. Verify the seat lock pins engage fully and the secondary stop is serviceable.
3. Check the rail hole edge distance against the wear limit (0.42 in minimum).

## Compliance Time
At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time-in-service, and before further flight if the
seat is found to slip on its rails.

## Corrective Action
Replace worn rails and locking pins with serviceable parts; re-inspect and record the
action in the aircraft technical log.
