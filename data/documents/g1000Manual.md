# Garmin G1000 NXi Pilot's Guide — C172S (Summary)

## System Overview
The G1000 NXi integrated flight deck comprises a Primary Flight Display (PFD) and a
Multifunction Display (MFD) driven by dual GIA 64 integrated avionics units.

## Primary Flight Display
- Airspeed, attitude, altitude, vertical speed, and heading tapes.
- Integrated GFC 700 autopilot mode annunciations across the top.

## Multifunction Display
- Moving map with terrain, traffic (when fitted), and weather overlays.
- Engine Indication System (EIS) strip on the left edge: RPM, fuel flow, oil
  temperature and pressure, EGT and CHT.

## Autopilot — GFC 700
- Modes: HDG, NAV, APR, VS, FLC, ALT.
- Flight Director command bars guide manual flight to the selected modes.

## Database Currency
Navigation and terrain databases must be current; check the database status page on the
MFD at power-up before IFR flight.
