# Maintenance Manual — Cessna 172S (G-ECHO)

## Inspection Programme
- 100-hour inspection: required when operated for hire; mirrors the annual scope.
- Annual inspection: complete airframe, engine, and avionics check by a licensed engineer.

## Engine — Lycoming IO-360-L2A
- Oil change interval: every 50 hours or 4 months, whichever comes first.
- Recommended oil: ashless dispersant SAE 20W-50.
- Spark plugs: rotate and gap-check at each 100-hour inspection.
- Recommended TBO (time between overhauls): 2000 hours.

## Landing Gear and Brakes
- Inspect tyres for wear and correct inflation (main 38 psi, nose 45 psi).
- Brake linings: replace when worn to minimum thickness 0.10 in.

## Lubrication
Lubricate flap tracks, control hinges, and the nose-gear shimmy damper per the chart at
each 100-hour inspection.

## Records
All maintenance actions must be recorded in the aircraft technical log with the engineer's
licence number, date, and aircraft total time in service.
