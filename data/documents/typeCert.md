# Type Certificate Data Sheet — Cessna 172 Series (Summary)

Type Certificate Holder: Textron Aviation (Cessna Aircraft Company).

## Applicable Model
- Model: 172S
- Engine: Lycoming IO-360-L2A
- Maximum continuous power: 180 BHP at 2700 RPM

## Certification Basis
Certified under the applicable airworthiness requirements for normal-category aeroplanes.
Approved for day and night VFR and IFR operation when equipped accordingly.

## Weights
- Maximum takeoff weight: 2550 lb
- Maximum landing weight: 2550 lb

## Propeller
Fixed-pitch, two-blade metal propeller; diameter 76 in (minimum 75 in after dressing).

## Fuel
- Approved grade: 100LL (blue) aviation gasoline
- Total usable fuel: 53 US gal (standard tanks)
