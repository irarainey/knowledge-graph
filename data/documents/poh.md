# Pilot's Operating Handbook — Cessna 172S Skyhawk (G-ECHO)

## Section 1 — General
The Cessna 172S is a four-seat, single-engine, high-wing fixed-gear aircraft powered by a
Lycoming IO-360-L2A engine rated at 180 BHP.

## Section 2 — Limitations
- Never-exceed speed (Vne): 163 KIAS
- Maximum structural cruising speed (Vno): 129 KIAS
- Maneuvering speed (Va) at 2550 lb: 105 KIAS
- Flaps extended speed range (Vfe): 85–110 KIAS
- Maximum takeoff weight: 2550 lb

## Section 3 — Emergency Procedures
Engine failure during flight: establish best glide at 68 KIAS, select a landing field,
check fuel selector BOTH, mixture RICH, ignition BOTH, and attempt a restart.

## Section 4 — Normal Procedures
Before takeoff run-up at 1800 RPM: check magnetos (max drop 150 RPM, max differential
50 RPM), suction 4.5–5.4 in Hg, and engine instruments in the green.

## Section 5 — Performance
Sea-level, ISA, 2550 lb: takeoff ground roll approximately 960 ft; total distance over a
50 ft obstacle approximately 1630 ft.
